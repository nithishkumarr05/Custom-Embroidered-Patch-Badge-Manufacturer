About Our Company

A Family Workshop,
Reborn as a Global Brand

StitchCraft began in 2012 in a small garage in East Los Angeles, founded by Maria and Daniel Reyes — a husband-and-wife team with a single industrial embroidery machine and a stubborn belief that patches could be art, not just accessories.

What started as a side hustle for local motorcycle clubs and scout troops quickly grew into something bigger. Word spread about the tight stitching, the rich thread colors, and the obsessive attention to detail. Today, our 12,000 sq. ft. facility produces over 500,000 patches a year for brands in 30+ countries — but we still treat every order like it's going on our own jacket.

"We don't just embroider logos. We stitch identity, memory, and belonging into every thread." — Maria Reyes, Founder

How We Make It

Manufacturing Process

Six precise steps from your artwork to your doorstep — all under one roof.

01

Artwork & Digitizing

Send us your logo in any format. Our in-house digitizers convert it into an embroidery file, optimizing stitch paths, density, and color sequencing.

02

Mockup Approval

You receive a digital proof showing thread colors, size, and stitch type. We revise until it's perfect — free and unlimited.

03

Thread Selection

Choose from 200+ Madeira polyester thread colors, metallic threads, and glow-in-the-dark options. All OEKO-TEX® certified.

04

Embroidery Production

Our 24-head industrial Tajima and Barudan machines stitch your patches onto premium twill, felt, or canvas backings at up to 1,000 stitches per minute.

05

Edge Finishing

Merrowed borders, laser-cut precision, or hot-cut sealing — we finish each edge to prevent fraying and give a clean, professional look.

06

QC & Shipping

Every patch is inspected by hand for stitch quality and color accuracy. Then packaged in eco-friendly materials and shipped with tracking worldwide.
