Badge Types

PVC · Metal · Embroidered

01 — PVC Badges

Durable & Waterproof

PVC badges are flexible, 3D, and built to withstand the elements. They're perfect for outdoor gear, tactical equipment, and any application where durability matters. With vibrant colors and precise detail, PVC badges make a bold statement.

  • Waterproof & UV resistant
  • 3D raised design
  • Velcro, adhesive, or pin backing
Get PVC Badge Quote
02 — Metal Badges

Premium & Elegant

Our metal badges are crafted from high-quality brass, copper, and nickel. With options for etching, embossing, and enamel fill, they offer a sophisticated look that elevates any brand or organization.

  • Brass, copper, nickel & more
  • Soft & hard enamel options
  • Magnetic, pin, or screw backing
Get Metal Badge Quote
03 — Embroidered Badges

Classic & Textured

Embroidered badges bring a timeless, tactile quality to your branding. With up to 12 thread colors and tight stitching, they're ideal for uniforms, caps, jackets, and any application where a traditional look is desired.

  • Up to 12 thread colors
  • Merrowed or laser-cut edges
  • Iron-on, sew-on, or Velcro backing
